01

Audio Ingress & DSP Kaiser Polyphase Resampling

Converts arbitrary variable-rate microphone inputs to standardized 16kHz forensic audio without phase distortion.

1. Kaiser Sinc Downsampling (48kHz → 16kHz)

Hardware microphones natively record at 48,000 Hz. Standard naive decimation causes spectral aliasing. VoiceShield uses polyphase sinc interpolation with a Kaiser window (β=5.0) ensuring pristine Nyquist attenuation.

resample_poly(x, up=1, down=3, window=('kaiser', 5.0))

2. Butterworth High-Pass & Hangover VAD

A 4th-order zero-phase Butterworth filter cuts DC offset below 40Hz. Frame-energy Voice Activity Detection (VAD) with 150ms pre-onset and 200ms hangover strips ambient room silence.

sosfiltfilt(butter(4, 40.0, 'highpass', fs=16000), x)
02

Orthogonal Multi-View Feature Extraction

Three complementary signal views capture spectral timbre, phase discontinuities, and biomechanical pitch.

A. Cepstral Stream

LFCC (Linear Frequency Cepstral Coefficients) with 60 linear filterbanks exposes high-frequency vocoder comb artifacts. Complemented by 40-band MFCC.

Tensor: [B, 2, 60, 400]

B. Spectral & Phase Stream

256-point STFT spectrogram coupled with instant Group Delay Phase Derivative (dθ/dt) to detect artificial vocoder frame phase boundary resets.

Tensor: [B, 2, 128, 400]

C. Glottal Biomechanics

5-period Glottal Jitter PPQ-5, 3-point Shimmer APQ-3, F0 contour dynamics with parabolic interpolation, and Harmonics-to-Noise Ratio (HNR dB).

Tensor: [B, 12] Vector
03

Dual-Stream ResNet & Multi-Head Self-Attention Pooling

Fuses cepstral, phase, and prosodic embeddings into a robust 128-dimensional biometric voiceprint.

Dual-Stream Residual Convolutions

Two parallel 2D ResNet branches process the Cepstral and Spectral-Phase representations through spatial residual blocks with batch normalization and GELU activations.

Conv2D -> BatchNorm2d -> GELU -> Residual Skip Connection

Multi-Head Self-Attention Pooling

Scaled dot-product attention computes dynamic temporal importance weights, collapsing time frames into a 128-dimensional forensic embedding vector.

Attention(Q,K,V) = softmax(Q K^T / √d_k) V -> Dense(128)

Physics-Guided Calibrator & Anti-Replay Shield

Guards against room reflections and phone loudspeaker attacks through acoustic physics invariants.

Sub-260 Hz Loudspeaker Cutoff Test

Mobile phone micro-transducers (10-15mm) physically attenuate frequencies below 260Hz. Ratios < 0.020 flag loudspeaker replay spoof attacks into laptop microphones.

sub_260_ratio = Σ_{f<260} |X(f)|² / Σ |X(f)|² < 0.020

Bounded Human Glottal Tremor

Natural involuntary vocal fold jitter strictly lies in [0.48%, 2.65%]. Jitter < 0.35% flags neural vocoders; jitter > 3.2% with phone acoustics confirms replay distortion.

0.48% ≤ Jitter_{PPQ} ≤ 2.65% (Natural Biomechanics)
